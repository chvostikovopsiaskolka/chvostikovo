## Chart definitions and limits

Monthly visits: one staff-only aggregate RPC per month, actual distinct dog/day visits including free, exclude test-source passes/reservations and linked non-attended reservations. Known completed financial dates can establish zero; dates before detailed coverage24Aug2026 are never derived from financial counters. Missing/future days are gaps. If detailed visits do not cover the existing monthly total, display both totals and a coverage warning; never rewrite attendance or spread a monthly count over days.

Yearly visits use existing monthly summaries. Current year ends at current month, marked in progress. Revenue uses the existing `revenueMonth` definition: single_count×existing25€, pass purchase_price on purchased_on, taxi_amount, hotel amount on date_from. Monthly chart has two daily series: single entries and sales of passes. Annual revenue includes taxi/hotel and is distinguished from lifetime total. Pass consumption is not another sale. Existing monetary records/prices/counters are unchanged. SVG charts reuse the current visual language, have axes/units/legends, gaps and touch/keyboard point detail. No per-point queries;60s cache and single-flight per month.
